What happens without it

I am writing it out in full, because it is the part nobody usually tells you.

The site stays online
Nothing gets switched off. It is yours, the files are yours, the domain is in your name.
Nobody does the updates
After a year or two the small things pile up: a form that no longer sends, an expired certificate, a photo that should have been replaced and wasn’t.
Changes are billed by the hour
They stay possible, but each becomes its own job: quote, wait, invoice. With care they are already included.
You can join later
No door closes. If you change your mind after a year, I look at the state of the site and we start.

For a site I did not build

I also take on sites built by others. First I look at what is there: how it is built, what runs underneath, what can be updated and what cannot. That costs {bestand} once, and I credit it against the care if you then take it.

The terms, in full

Minimum term of twelve months

After that you cancel whenever you like, at the end of the month. No automatic renewal you notice too late.

No increases out of nowhere

If the price ever changes, you hear about it first and you decide.

The site stays yours

Even if you cancel. You get the access details and carry on alone or with someone else.

One person to talk to

You write to me and I answer. No ticket system, no hotline.

Shall I look after it?

Tell me which site it is and I will tell you what it needs. If I build the site, care simply comes with the quote — you do not have to decide now.
